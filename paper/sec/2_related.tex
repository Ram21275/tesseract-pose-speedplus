\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Spacecraft pose estimation.}
Early learning-based methods regress or classify pose directly~\cite{sharma2019spn} or detect keypoints and solve PnP~\cite{park2019krn}. SPEED+~\cite{park2022speedplus} shifted the focus to the synthetic-to-real gap. SPNv2~\cite{park2024spnv2} combines multi-task learning, heavy style augmentation and online refinement, and unsupervised domain adaptation has also been used~\cite{perezvillar2022vpu}. Direct regression with Vision Transformers is fast~\cite{ancey2026fastpose}. SPACE-HOP~\cite{dutta2026spacehop} pre-trains a ViT with a joint-embedding predictive objective and classifies rotation over a Hopf-fibration grid with tangent-space offsets. It reports a characteristic W-shaped error histogram with a secondary peak at $180^\circ$, which we analyse as a mode-selection problem. Unlike these works, we keep the backbones frozen and train only small heads.

\paragraph{Discrete and distributional rotation prediction.}
Uniform $SO(3)$ grids can be built from the Hopf fibration~\cite{yershova2010hopf} or by recursive tesseract subdivision~\cite{kurz2017tesseract}; we adapt the latter to cell-centre representatives with unique paths. Grid- and implicit-density methods model multi-modal rotation posteriors, either through MLP scores over grids~\cite{murphy2021implicitpdf,hofer2023hyperposepdf} or through equivariant spherical features~\cite{klee2023image2sphere}; parametric alternatives include Bingham mixtures~\cite{deng2022bingham}. SpyroPose~\cite{haugaard2023spyropose} evaluates a hierarchical $SE(3)$ pyramid sparsely, coarse to fine. LiePose~\cite{hsiao2024liepose} applies score-based diffusion on $SE(3)$ and handles symmetric objects well. Continuous rotation regression has known representation issues~\cite{zhou2019continuity}. Riemannian~\cite{chen2024rfm} and discrete~\cite{gat2024dfm} flow matching provide generative alternatives; we test both as refiners and report them as negative results.

\paragraph{Consistency, test-time augmentation and distillation.}
Averaging predictions over transformed inputs is a standard way to obtain test-time robustness. Data distillation~\cite{radosavovic2018data} turns such multi-transform ensembles into training targets, a form of knowledge distillation~\cite{hinton2015distilling}. Equivariant architectures build rotation consistency into the network~\cite{klee2023image2sphere}. Our orbit voting is a test-time augmentation over in-plane rotations, and our orbit distillation is a data-distillation variant. What is specific here is the exact camera-roll transform for crops, scoring with hierarchical tree likelihoods rather than averaging point estimates, and the target: a diagnosed flip-selection failure under domain shift.
