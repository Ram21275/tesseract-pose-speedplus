# Qualitative figures: rotation and translation on every application

Each figure comes as a PDF (vector, for LaTeX) and a 300-dpi PNG. All of them are generated by `scripts/fig_qualitative.py` from per-image predictions of the **finalised configurations**.

## Where the predictions come from
- The predictions are seed-0 re-runs, produced by `scripts/run_queue18.sh` with `--save-preds`.
- The source runs are listed in `sources.txt`.
- Each re-run reproduces the seed-0 metrics already in the paper exactly (difference 0.0 on every row):
  - T-LESS: EXP-129 `RUN-20261103-162450`;
  - YCB-V: EXP-129 `RUN-20261103-165739`;
  - SYMSOL-T: EXP-126 `RUN-20261030-141752`;
  - SPEED+: EXP-124 `RUN-20261028-231916` (GT crops) and `RUN-20261029-232454` (detected crops).

## Configurations shown

| Application | Rotation | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| T-LESS | Multi-path (A2) PoE trees **before distillation** + orbit voting. This is the Table A 6D-cell (‡) configuration; the distilled students have no attached translation block. | DINOv3 block, orbit mean. It is also used for the allocentric→camera conversion. |
| YCB-V | Synthetic-trained DINOv3 tree, 1 view | DINOv3 block, view 0. It is also used for the conversion. |
| SYMSOL-T | Multi-path DINOv3 tree + orbit voting | DINOv3 block, orbit mean (scene units) |
| SPEED+ | S1: distilled PoE students + orbit voting (pre-registered product rule) | Joint DINOv3 block, orbit mean |

No ground-truth pose information is used for any prediction drawn. The crops are ground truth except in `speedplus_detected_quantiles`.

## How examples were chosen (fixed rule, no hand-picking)
- **Quantile grids:** the test split of seed 0. For each object, shape or domain, take the images whose final rotation error is closest to the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile, plus the worst image.
- **Overview:** the median image of each object.
- **Orbit-voting example:** among SPEED+ lightbox images where 1-view error > 150° and orbit error < 10°, the one whose orbit error is closest to that group's median.
- **SO(3) examples:** the median image of each SYMSOL-T shape, and of the first four T-LESS objects that have a symmetry set.
- **Record of choices:** every chosen image, with its rotation error (final and 1-view) and translation error, is in `selection.csv`.

## Drawing conventions
- **Colours:** green is ground truth, magenta is prediction.
- **Box and axes:** each pose is drawn as the object's 3D bounding box (from its model points; SPEED+ uses the Tango keypoints) plus its body axes (x red, y green, z blue). GT axes are solid and predicted axes dashed.
- **Translation:** the cross marks the projected object origin, so the gap between the green and magenta crosses is the translation error in the image.
- **Prediction:** each predicted box uses the predicted rotation **and** the predicted translation.
- **Symmetric objects:** the GT is drawn as its symmetry-equivalent closest to the prediction. This is the same physical pose; it is drawn that way so the boxes are comparable.
- **Error labels:** E_R is the symmetry-aware rotation error in the camera frame; E_T is the translation error.
- **SPEED+ contrast:** SPEED+ crops get a percentile contrast stretch, for display only.

## Things to know before citing numbers from these figures
- **Pooling:** the in-figure numbers are seed 0, pooled over images. The paper's tables are object-balanced means over 3 seeds, so they differ. For example, YCB-V seed 0 pooled is 6.2°, while Table A reports 12.9 ± 6.9° over 3 seeds. Cite the tables for numbers and use the figures for illustration.
- **No T-LESS orbit-voting figure:**
  - Under the same rule only 2 of 2,345 seed-0 T-LESS test images are repaired flips, and 4 are broken.
  - On T-LESS, orbit voting improves the bulk of the distribution rather than repairing flips; see `rotation_error_cdf`.

## Regenerating the figures

2. Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/fig_qualitative.py` (CPU, a few minutes).
